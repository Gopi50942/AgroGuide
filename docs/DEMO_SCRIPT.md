# AgroGuide Official Demonstration Walkthrough Script

## 1. Farmer Journey
1. **Landing & Login**: Open `/login` and select Demo Farmer (`Gopi S`).
2. **Dashboard & Weather**: Review NDVI satellite vegetation health, weather forecasts, and daily tasks.
3. **Unified Passbook**: Navigate to `/passbook` to view consolidated crop, soil, sales, and scheme history. Print or export PDF.
4. **Tamil Voice Assistant**: Click the microphone icon to ask voice questions in Tamil (e.g. "இன்று மழை வருமா?").
5. **Crop Doctor & Market**: Scan diseased leaf or check live mandi prices at Coimbatore APMC.

## 2. Field Officer Journey
1. **Officer Hub**: Open `/officer` to view pending farm inspections and CCE trial schedules.
2. **Offline Inspection**: Record geo-tagged crop observation with tamper-evident EXIF data.
3. **Emergency Agri SOS**: Dispatch officer for Alandurai flood waterlogging distress request.

## 3. State Command Center Journey
1. **Government Console**: Open `/government-console` to view district telemetry, village digital twins, and decision priorities.
2. **Decision Priorities**: Review high-priority DAP fertilizer buffer stock allocations.
3. **Open Data & NOC**: Review open data feeds under `/api/open-data/v1/crop-acreage` and system health under `/state-control-room`.
